# Android updater third-party notices

Applies to the locked runtime artifacts of the Android updater. Runtime coordinates below come from the locked
`releaseRuntimeClasspath`; build plugins and test-only dependencies are excluded.
Archive LICENSE/NOTICE texts are preserved below. Common license texts are
included once when byte-identical. POM-only platform/BOM metadata has no APK code.

AndroidX, Jackson, Apache Commons, Kotlin, coroutines, serialization, JetBrains
annotations and Guava listenablefuture use Apache-2.0. SLF4J uses MIT.
Nordic mcumgr and BLE published POMs declare BSD-3-Clause. Published MCUmgr
sources additionally retain Apache-2.0 headers for Runtime Inc. (2017–2018),
Intellinium SAS (2014–present), and Nordic Semiconductor (2018); both licenses
are retained. Nordic BLE license copyright is 2015 Nordic Semiconductor.

Guava listenablefuture 1.0 has no archive license entry; its Maven POM inherits
Apache-2.0 from guava-parent 26.0-android. Apache Commons and Jackson CBOR inherit
Apache-2.0 from their POM parents; their archive texts below corroborate this.
